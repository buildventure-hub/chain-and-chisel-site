About Chain & Chisel

The work is rooted in family craft, practice, and a lot of sawdust.

Anthony grew up around chainsaw art through his uncle, then kept coming back to carving until it became something worth putting out into the world. Every piece sold here helped turn that from a side pursuit into real commissioned work.

My uncle has been a chainsaw artist for more than 30 years, so carving was always close by. Every visit to Uncle Frank turned into another excuse to pick up a saw and try something new. Some of the earlier pieces were rougher than the newer work, but that is part of the story too: each carving taught the next one how to be better.

The pieces shown on this site have all sold, and that momentum is what pushed Chain & Chisel into commissioned work. If you have a carving in mind, a stump that deserves another life, or just want to ask whether something is possible, reach out and start the conversation.
